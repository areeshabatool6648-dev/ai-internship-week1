DeskNest is a small business invoicing and accounting platform. It offers invoicing, expense tracking, and integrations with QuickBooks and other accounting tools.

DeskNest has three customer segments: freelancers (individual users), small businesses (teams of 2-50 people), and enterprise (teams of 200+ people).

Common features requested by freelancers include a mobile app for sending invoices on the go, and lower pricing tiers for individual users.

Small business customers frequently report issues with QuickBooks integration reliability and slow customer support response times, sometimes exceeding 48 hours.

Enterprise customers need role-based permissions, single sign-on (SSO) support, and higher API rate limits for automated workflows. Onboarding documentation for new team members is currently limited.

The support team aims to respond to all tickets within 24 hours, though this target is not always met during high-volume periods.
